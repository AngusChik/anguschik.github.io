Problem

A retail pharmacy needed a way to track stock, process orders, and manage customer records without relying on spreadsheets or paper — the challenge was building something reliable enough to trust with real inventory.

Process

  • Modelled products with barcodes, brand, pricing, stock levels, and category
  • Built order and order-detail relational structure for accurate transaction history
  • Used Django admin as the primary interface to keep the build lean and focused

Learnings

Data integrity at the model layer matters more than UI polish in operational tools. Getting the relational structure right early — especially order details and stock quantities — avoided significant rework later.

02 Full-Stack 2025

A Django-based inventory and point-of-sale system for a retail pharmacy — products, orders, customers, and stock in one place.

The system tracks products by barcode, brand, unit size, and category — with stock quantities updated through order processing. Orders link to line-item detail records and customers, giving a full transaction history without needing a third-party platform.

Outcome

A working backend system for real pharmacy inventory and order tracking.
