Problem

Cyclists in the Toronto area had no tool that balanced both safety risk and route quality — existing mapping apps optimise for speed or distance, not for the experience or danger level of the ride.

Process

  • Integrated OpenRouteService for multi-route generation and MapLibre GL for rendering
  • Built custom risk and scenic scoring functions applied per-route segment
  • Added QR code sharing and geo autocomplete for a complete user flow

Learnings

Geographic data is messy. Normalising route data across sources before applying scoring was the hardest part — the scoring logic itself was straightforward once the inputs were clean and consistent.

03 Web App 2025

A React cycling route planner that scores paths by risk and scenic quality — built for riders who care about more than just distance.

The app fetches multiple route options via OpenRouteService, renders them on a MapLibre GL map, and scores each one using custom haversine-based risk and scenic algorithms. Riders can compare routes by insight summary, share a result via QR code, and search locations with geo autocomplete.

Outcome

A functional route planner that surfaces safety and scenic tradeoffs cyclists actually care about.
